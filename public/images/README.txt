========================================
HOW TO REPLACE PHOTOS FOR YOUR INVITATION
========================================

Just place your own photos in this folder (public/images/) with these exact names:

1. couple-hero.jpg       -> Main couple photo on the front page (Aspect ratio 4:5 or portrait)
2. auditorium-venue.jpg  -> Photo of NAS Auditorium, Kumbidi (Aspect ratio 16:9 or landscape)
3. gallery-1.jpg         -> Moments we cherish - Photo 1
4. gallery-2.jpg         -> Moments we cherish - Photo 2
5. gallery-3.jpg         -> Moments we cherish - Photo 3
6. gallery-4.jpg         -> Moments we cherish - Photo 4
7. gallery-5.jpg         -> Moments we cherish - Photo 5
8. gallery-6.jpg         -> Moments we cherish - Photo 6

If you want to add background music mp3:
Place an audio file named `background-romantic.mp3` inside `public/audio/`.

All other details (names, dates, events, RSVP, phone numbers) can be edited in:
`src/data/weddingData.ts`
